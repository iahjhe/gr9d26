Foundations

Key Geometry Terminologies

Angle Bisector

A ray, line, or segment that divides an angle into two equal, congruent angles.

Perpendicular Lines (⟂)

Two lines or segments that intersect at a 90° right angle.

CPCTC

Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles are Congruent.

SAS Postulate

Side-Angle-Side: 2 sides & the included angle are equal.

LA Theorem

Leg-Acute: 1 leg & 1 acute angle of a right triangle are equal.

Complementary Angles

Two adjacent or non-adjacent angles whose sum equals exactly 90°.

A · Angle bisector · Steps 4–6

Adjacent angles prove BC is a bisector

4. Adjacent angles

Pair 1: ∠1 and ∠5Pair 2: ∠3 and ∠4

5. Relationship: congruent, since they are corresponding parts of the congruent triangles (CPCTC).

m∠1 = m∠5
m∠ABD = m∠1 + m∠5 = m∠1 + m∠1
m∠1 = ½ (m∠ABD)
m∠3 = m∠4 → m∠3 = ½ (m∠ACD)
Each angle is half the whole angle, so BC bisects ∠ABD and ∠ACD. Hence BC is an angle bisector.
A · Angle bisector · Example 2 (ASA)

Same idea on ΔXYZ and ΔXYC

Given two congruent triangles by the ASA (Angle-Side-Angle) Postulate, CPCTC gives:

∠3 ≅ ∠5
XZ ≅ XC
YZ ≅ YC

Common side XY shared by both triangles. Adjacent angles: ∠1 & ∠4, ∠2 & ∠6 — congruent, so XY divides ∠ZXC and ∠ZYC equally.

m∠ZXC = m∠1 + m∠4 = 2·m∠1
m∠ZYC = m∠2 + m∠6 = 2·m∠2
m∠1 = ½(m∠ZXC), m∠2 = ½(m∠ZYC)
B · Perpendicular lines · Two right triangles (LA)

Complementary angles force a 90° angle

By the LA (Leg-Acute) Theorem and CPCTC:

∠AXY ≅ ∠ADC   AX ≅ XD   YX ≅ CD

Place the triangles so the vertices labeled X coincide.

m∠A + m∠AXY + 90° = 180°
m∠A + m∠AXY = 90°
m∠AXY = 90° − m∠A
Since ∠A ≅ ∠CXD: m∠AXY + m∠CXD = 90°
∠AXY and ∠CXD are complementary angles.
Their sum is 90°, so AX ⟂ XD by the definition of perpendicularity.

B · Perpendicular lines · Reference

Right Triangle Congruence Theorems

LA Theorem

Leg-Acute: One leg and one acute angle match.

LL Theorem

Leg-Leg: Both legs match (equivalent to SAS).

HA Theorem

Hypotenuse-Acute: Hypotenuse and one acute angle match (AAS).

HL Theorem

Hypotenuse-Leg: Hypotenuse and one leg match.

B · Perpendicular lines · Example 2

Common side BC is perpendicular to the base

CPCTC:  BA ≅ BD   AC ≅ DC

Common side: BC
Adjacent pairs: ∠ABC & ∠DBC, and ∠ACB & ∠DCB

A. ∠ABC ≅ ∠DBC, so BC is the angle bisector of the vertex angle.

B. ∠ACB and ∠DCB are congruent and both right angles: BC is perpendicular to the base AD.

Recap

Two tools from congruent triangles

Angle bisector

Join the triangles along a common side. Congruent adjacent angles each equal half of the whole angle, so the common side bisects it.

Perpendicular lines

Congruent right triangles give complementary angles (sum 90°) or two congruent adjacent right angles, so the segments are perpendicular.
